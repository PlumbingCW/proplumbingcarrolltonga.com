How to tell it is time to replace

A tank that weeps from the body or the bottom seam has corroded through, and no part will fix that. Rusty hot water that is clear on the cold side, a tank that is past its second decade, and repairs that now come twice a year all point the same direction. The Department of Energy says most storage water heaters last 10 to 15 years.

Age is written on the rating plate on the side of the tank. The serial number encodes the month and year, and the code layout varies by brand, so we read it for you on arrival. If you want to look first, the plate lists capacity, fuel type, and input rating, which are the three numbers we need to quote a like-for-like swap.

Tank failed? Emergency replacement starts with a shutoff

Turn off the cold water valve above the tank and shut off the power or gas control. Then run a hose from the drain valve to a floor drain or the yard if you can reach it safely. Draining the tank lowers the pressure that feeds the leak.

Move anything stored on the floor beside the heater, especially cardboard. Call (770) 214-4545 and tell us the tank size and whether it is gas or electric. With that we can bring the right unit on the first trip when stock allows.

What a replacement day looks like

Most tank swaps are done in a single visit.

  1. Confirm the match.

    We check the old unit's capacity, fuel, venting, and where it sits, then agree on the new model with you.

  2. Drain and disconnect.

    We shut off water and fuel, drain the tank, and disconnect the supply, gas or electric, and vent.

  3. Set and connect.

    The new tank goes in with new flex connectors, a shutoff valve, and a relief valve discharge line piped to a safe place.

  4. Test and walk through.

    We fill it, bleed the air, light or power it, check for leaks, set the temperature, and show you the controls.

Permits and paperwork in Carrollton

The City of Carrollton issues building, electrical, HVAC, plumbing, gas, and pool permits, and it says permits are picked up in person by the state card holder. Whether a water heater swap needs one depends on the jurisdiction your home sits in, and homes outside the city limits may fall under a different office.

We tell you which office to ask and we do not skip a permit that applies. Ask before you let anyone start the work. A plumber who says a permit is never needed is not telling you the whole story.

Code and permits for this work

Georgia's Department of Community Affairs publishes the state's amendments to the International Plumbing Code (IPC), the standard that plumbing work in Georgia is measured against, and the City of Carrollton issues plumbing permits through its permits office. A replacement tank brings its own code items, including the relief valve and its discharge pipe, and a permit may apply.

What drives the price of a new water heater

The unit is only part of the bill. Installation conditions change the total more than most people expect.

  • Tank size and fuel type.
  • Efficiency tier and warranty length of the unit.
  • Venting changes or a new gas line fitting.
  • Electrical work if you are changing fuel or upsizing.
  • Access, such as an attic platform or a tight crawlspace.
  • Whether permit fees and old unit disposal are part of the job.

We give a clear price before work starts so you can compare. Many insurance policies cover water damage from a sudden tank failure but not the cost of the new tank.
