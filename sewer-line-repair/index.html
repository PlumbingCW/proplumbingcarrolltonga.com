Emergency Sewer Line Repair in Carrollton, GA

A sewer line fails slowly and then all at once. Roots pry joints open, soil shifts, old pipe cracks, and one day every drain in the house backs up. We find the failure with a camera, explain the repair options, and fix the line in Carrollton and nearby towns. The 24/7 emergency line is open for sewage backups.

Sewer emergency: sewage in the house or yard

Stop all water use. That means toilets, showers, laundry, and the dishwasher. Keep children and pets away from standing sewage and open windows if the smell is strong. Do not try to clear it with drain chemicals.

Call (770) 214-4545 and tell us what is backing up and where. If the cleanout in the yard is overflowing, the blockage is in the line from the house to the street. If water is rising only inside, the blockage may be at a fixture or branch. A camera run after the line is clear shows whether the pipe itself has failed.

How sewer lines fail

Older homes may have clay, cast iron, or fiber conduit pipe that cracks, separates, or collapses with age. Newer homes use PVC, which is durable but can sag when the trench was not packed well. Tree roots enter at any open joint and grow into a mass that catches paper and grease.

Symptoms include slow drains in the whole house, gurgling toilets, sewer odors in the yard, wet or sunken spots along the line, and repeated stoppages. One backup can be a clog. Three in a year is a pipe problem.

How a sewer repair project runs

The order matters because the camera decides the scope.

  1. Clear and camera.

    We clear the line so the camera can travel, then record the pipe from the cleanout to the street.

  2. Locate and measure.

    The camera transmitter lets us mark the depth and position of each defect on the surface.

  3. Call 811 and plan.

    Before any digging, utilities are marked. We schedule the repair and explain the method.

  4. Repair and retest.

    We repair the section, backfill, and run the camera again to confirm the line is clear and sealed.

Digging safely in Carroll County

Georgia 811 connects people planning to dig with the utilities that have lines underground, and its service is free. The call takes minutes and prevents a gas line or electric strike. We make the call as part of any sewer excavation, and you should make it before any mechanized digging of your own.

Where a sewer line runs under a driveway, patio, or mature tree, replacement can be disruptive. In those cases pipe lining may spare the surface, if the pipe is in a condition to accept it. See our hydro jetting page for how lines are cleaned before lining.

What to do before the crew arrives at a sewer problem

Stop using water in the house. Keep children and pets away from any area where sewage has surfaced, and do not walk through it barefoot.

Call 811 before anyone digs on the property. Do not pour chemical drain cleaner into a failing line.

Code and permits for this work

Georgia's Department of Community Affairs publishes the state's amendments to the International Plumbing Code (IPC), the standard that plumbing work in Georgia is measured against, and the City of Carrollton issues plumbing permits through its permits office. Replacing a sewer line is code work that normally needs a permit and an inspection, and 811 must be called before digging.

What changes the price of sewer line repair

Sewer work varies widely, and the camera is what lets us give a real number.

  • The length of pipe that must be repaired or replaced.
  • Depth of the line and the type of soil.
  • What sits on top: lawn, driveway, concrete, or landscaping.
  • Repair method: spot, lining, or replacement.
  • Permit requirements, which depend on your jurisdiction.
  • Emergency timing.
