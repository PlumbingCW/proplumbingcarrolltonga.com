If a pipe bursts or a leak starts in Bowdon, do this before we arrive

Distance from the shop means a few minutes of waiting, so use them to limit the damage. These four steps apply to most calls from a Bowdon house.

Close the main valve.

Kill power to wet rooms.

Switch off breakers for wet areas from a dry spot, and stay out of standing water near outlets.

Clear the immediate area.

Lift rugs and move boxes so water has nowhere to soak in.
