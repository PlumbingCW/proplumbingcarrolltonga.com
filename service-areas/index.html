Plumbing Service Areas Around Carrollton, GA

Our base is on US Highway 27 in Carrollton. From here the emergency line covers the city and the towns around it, and Bowdon to the west has its own page.

Carrollton and its ZIP codes

The ZIP codes 30116 and 30117 are the two standard ZIP codes for the city. Our own address on US Highway 27 is in 30116, and City Hall and the water filter plant carry 30117. We also dispatch to 30112, 30118, and 30119, which are Carrollton ZIP codes used for post office boxes and specific organizations. Whatever the ZIP code on your mail, the question that matters is the street address, so give it to the dispatcher.

Carrollton is the Carroll County seat, and the area includes the University of West Georgia, and the downtown square. Houses range from older homes near downtown to newer subdivisions, and the city's own service line inventory asks owners of structures built in 1989 or earlier to check their water lines.

Bowdon: a separate town with its own page

Bowdon, ZIP code 30108, is about 12 miles west of Carrollton on Georgia 166. It has its own water department and its own character, so it has a dedicated page describing how emergency calls work there. Read the Bowdon emergency plumber page for details, or call with a Bowdon address.

How we decide where to dispatch

Emergency work needs a realistic response, so we think in terms of driving time on real roads, not straight-line distance. US Highway 27 runs north and south through Carrollton to Bremen and Interstate 20, and Georgia 166 runs west to Bowdon. Villa Rica sits along US 78 and Interstate 20 on the east side of the county.

If your town is not on this list, call anyway. We tell you honestly whether we can reach you the same day, and if we cannot we say so.

Not sure which service you need?

Start from what is happening. A backed-up drain points to drain cleaning, a cold shower to water heater repair, and an unexplained water bill to leak detection. The full service list has every option.

What to tell us about your location

Give the street address and, if you are in a rural area, the nearest crossroads or landmark. If your home is on a private road or behind a gate, say so, and give a number where we can reach you if the call drops.

Tell us whether the property is on city water and sewer or on a well and septic, since the right equipment differs. If you are unsure, check a recent bill or the utility name on the meter.

Roads, water, and why the town matters

Carrollton sits where US Highway 27 meets the roads that run to Bowdon, Villa Rica, and Whitesburg, so most of the area is a short drive from the base on US 27. The city supplies treated water from the Little Tallapoosa River and its reservoirs, and customers outside city limits may be served by another utility or by a private well. Check which applies before you call.

That matters because the first shutoff differs. On city water, the meter box near the street holds a utility valve and your own main shutoff is at the house. On a well, the pressure tank and pump control the supply. The dispatcher walks you through the right one.

Planned work in the nearby towns

Emergency calls are only part of the work. Homeowners in the nearby towns also book water heater installs, repipes, and fixture upgrades. Planned work lets us schedule a day that suits you and bring the right parts, which saves a second trip.

If you are planning a remodel, call early. Rough-in decisions made before the walls close are far cheaper than changes made after.

Landmarks that help us find you

Rural addresses can be hard to find at night. When you call, name the nearest crossroads, a church, a school, or a business we can look up. In Carroll County, many roads share a name with a nearby town, so the cross street and the ZIP code help the dispatcher pick the right one.

If your driveway is long or hidden, say so. Turn on an outside light and, if it is safe, have someone wait at the road to guide the technician in.
