What an emergency plumber in Carrollton does

An emergency plumber is a licensed plumber who answers the phone when water, sewage, or a failed water heater cannot wait until morning. The job is to stop the damage first, usually by closing a valve or clearing a blockage, then to repair the cause. We staff a 24/7 emergency line for that purpose and offer same-day appointments when available.

Most calls fall into a few groups. A pipe has split and water is spreading. A main drain has stopped and the lowest fixture is overflowing. A water heater has started leaking or gone cold. A toilet will not stop running or has overflowed. Each has a first step you can take in minutes, and we walk you through it on the phone.

Before the plumber arrives: five minutes that limit the damage

Water causes most of the cost in a plumbing emergency, so the first minutes matter more than anything we do later. These steps are safe for any homeowner and they apply to nearly every emergency call.

Close the main shutoff.

Find the valve near the meter or where the line enters the house and turn it clockwise until it stops.

Stop using drains.

If sewage or gray water is coming up, stop flushing, showering, and running the washer or dishwasher.

Protect electrical.

Stay out of wet areas near outlets and appliances, and switch off power only from a dry spot.

Clear the area.

Move rugs, boxes, and furniture off wet floors so they can dry and so the crew can work.

Call and describe it.

Plumbing in Carrollton: what local homes face

Carrollton is the Carroll County seat, about 45 miles west of Atlanta near the Alabama line. Housing here spans neighborhoods built decades ago to recent subdivisions. The City of Carrollton is building a water service line inventory and asks owners of structures built in 1989 or earlier to check their lines. If your house is in that group, the pipes under the floor and in the yard deserve a look. Our repiping page explains the options.

The city draws its water from the Little Tallapoosa River and three reservoirs, Lake Buckhorn, Sharpe Creek Reservoir, and Lake Carroll, and treats it at the City of Carrollton Water Filter Plant. Its published water quality report did not list a hardness figure, so test your own tap if you want to know. If you are weighing a tankless heater, the tankless water heater page covers how scale affects it.

Winters here are mild but not freeze-proof. January's average low is about 31.6 degrees, and Carroll County's hazard data lists four winter weather events between 2015 and early 2019. That is enough for unprotected pipes in garages, crawlspaces, and exterior walls to split. A cold night is when most burst pipe repairs happen.

Plumbing work in Georgia is measured against the International Plumbing Code with the state amendments published by the Georgia Department of Community Affairs. According to the City of Carrollton, plumbing permits are issued by the city and picked up by the state card holder, so ask any plumber who will handle the permit for your job.
