Emergency Garbage Disposal Repair in Carrollton, GA

A disposal that hums and will not turn is jammed. One that is silent has lost power. One that leaks under the sink needs a seal or a replacement. We diagnose all three in Carrollton kitchens and install new disposals when repair is not worth it. A leak under the sink is worth a call to the 24/7 emergency line.

Disposal emergency: leaking or jammed under the sink

Turn off the switch first. Never put a hand into a disposal, even when it is off. If the unit is humming and will not spin, a hard object is jammed against the grinding plate. Look for a small hex socket in the center of the underside and turn it with a wrench to free it.

If water leaks from the disposal, turn off the switch, place a bucket underneath, and call (770) 214-4545. A leak from the body usually means the housing seal has failed and the unit needs replacing.

What the symptom points to

A disposal gives clear clues.

Symptom.Usual cause.Usual fix.
Hums, will not spin.Jam against the grinding plate.Free the flywheel and clear the jam.
Silent, no hum.Tripped reset button or no power.Press reset, check the outlet.
Leaks from the top.Failed sink flange seal.Reseat the flange with new putty or gasket.
Leaks from the side or bottom.Cracked housing or failed seal.Replace the unit.
Drains slowly.Clog past the disposal.Clear the trap and drain line.

What not to put down a disposal

Grease, coffee grounds, eggshells, pasta, rice, and fibrous vegetables like celery and corn husks cause most disposal clogs and the drain stoppages that follow. They do not grind to a liquid and they settle in the trap or the line beyond it. Cold water flowing for 15 seconds after use helps move small bits along.

If a disposal keeps backing up the sink even when it runs, the clog is past it, and our drain cleaning service is the right fix. A new disposal will not cure a blocked drain.

Replacing a disposal

When repair makes no sense we install a new unit.

  1. Choose the size.

    A larger motor grinds more and runs quieter. We match the unit to your sink flange and your use.

  2. Remove the old one.

    We disconnect the power, the drain, and the dishwasher hose, then lift out the old unit.

  3. Mount and connect.

    We seat the sink flange, mount the new unit, and reconnect the drain and dishwasher line.

  4. Test.

    We run water through it, check every joint for drips, and show you the reset button.

Why the dishwasher matters here

The dishwasher drain hose connects to the disposal, usually through a small knockout plug inside the unit. If the plug was left in during a previous install, the dishwasher cannot drain. If the disposal clogs, dirty water can back up into the dishwasher.

We check this connection on every disposal install. If a dishwasher leaves standing water at the bottom, ask about it when you call. See our plumbing installation page for other kitchen fixtures we connect.

Care that makes a disposal last

Run cold water before, during, and for about 15 seconds after grinding. Cold water keeps grease firm so it is chopped and carried on instead of smearing the pipe wall. Feed scraps gradually rather than in one load.

Grind a few ice cubes now and then to knock buildup off the chamber. Citrus peels freshen it. Avoid bones, fruit pits, and anything stringy. These habits keep the motor from straining and reduce clogs in the line beyond.

Code and permits for this work

Georgia's Department of Community Affairs publishes the state's amendments to the International Plumbing Code (IPC), the standard that plumbing work in Georgia is measured against, and the City of Carrollton issues plumbing permits through its permits office. A disposal install touches the drain trap and the power connection, so the trap arrangement follows the code and the wiring may need an electrician.

What changes the price of a disposal job

A repair is inexpensive. A replacement depends on the motor and the install.

  • Whether the unit can be freed or needs replacing.
  • The horsepower and quality of a new disposal.
  • Electrical access under the sink.
  • Whether a new sink flange or drain parts are needed.
  • After-hours dispatch.

You see a clear price before work starts. If the disposal is older than about a decade and failing, we explain why replacing it often costs less than repeated repairs.
