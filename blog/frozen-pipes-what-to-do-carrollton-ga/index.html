Frozen Pipes: What to Do in Carrollton, GA

A frozen pipe is a warning. The ice has not yet split the pipe, and the next hour decides whether it does. This guide covers how to tell a frozen pipe from a blocked one, how to thaw it safely, what never to do, and how to protect pipes before the next cold night in Carrollton.

By the Carrollton Pro Plumbing team

Call (770) 214-4545

How to tell the pipe is frozen

The clearest sign is that a faucet runs a trickle or nothing, while the others in the house work. Frost on the outside of an exposed pipe, a bulge in a pipe, or a hissing sound can show where the ice is. Pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, garages, and attics freeze first.

If one faucet is dry, check whether the cold is the cause. A frozen pipe is usually in a cold zone. A dry hot tap with working cold points to the water heater, not ice. If the whole house has no water, the main line or the meter may be frozen, and you should call the utility and a plumber.

Thawing a frozen pipe safely

Go slowly. A fast thaw can crack a pipe that the ice has already stressed.

  1. Open the faucet.

    Leave the tap open so water can flow as the ice melts and relieves pressure.

  2. Apply gentle heat.

    Use a hair dryer, a heating pad, or warm towels, starting near the faucet and working back toward the ice.

  3. Check along the pipe.

    Look for splits and leaks as the ice melts. A pipe that has split will leak as soon as it thaws.

  4. Keep the heat on.

    Open cabinet doors and keep the house warm until the weather moderates.

Do and do not

Some well-meant fixes cause fires and burst pipes.

Do.Do not.
Use a hair dryer or heating pad.Use a torch, a heat gun, or any open flame.
Keep a faucet open while thawing.Leave the tap closed and heat a sealed pipe.
Know where the main shutoff is.Wait to find the valve until water is spraying.
Call a plumber if you cannot reach the ice.Break open walls to look for the frozen section.

If the pipe has already burst

Close the main shutoff right away, and open a faucet at the lowest point of the house to drain the lines. Switch off power to wet areas only from a dry spot. Move belongings away from the water and photograph the damage for your insurer. Then call us.

Our burst pipe repair team answers the emergency line 24/7 and starts by locating the split. Do not wait for the water to stop on its own. A split pipe will keep leaking once the ice thaws, and the thaw can release a lot of water at once.

Prevention before the next freeze

Insulate pipes in garages, crawlspaces, and exterior walls with foam sleeves. Disconnect hoses from outdoor spigots and cover them. On the coldest nights, open cabinet doors under sinks on outside walls and let a faucet drip. Keep the thermostat steady, even if you are away.

Know where your main shutoff is before you need it. If you plan to travel in winter, ask a neighbor to check the house. A house that loses heat for a day in a hard freeze is at risk even if the pipes were fine when you left.

Where pipes freeze first in Georgia homes

In this part of the state, the first pipes to freeze are the ones outside the heated envelope. Hose bibs, irrigation lines, pipes in a vented crawlspace, and supply lines in a garage or on a north wall are the usual casualties. Water heaters in unheated garages can also freeze at the connections.

Pipes run through an exterior wall behind a kitchen sink are another common spot, because the cabinet keeps warm house air away from the pipe. Opening the cabinet door on a freezing night lets room air reach it.

After the thaw

Once water flows, check every joint you can reach for drips and look at ceilings and walls below for stains. A pipe can crack without leaking until pressure returns. Watch the water meter for an hour with everything off, using the steps in our meter guide, to confirm nothing is leaking.

If anything looks wrong, call before the damage spreads. A small split caught early is a short repair. A split left running behind a wall can soak insulation and drywall.

Carrollton, GA note: how often it gets cold enough

According to published climate normals, Carrollton's January mean daily low is about 31.6 degrees, which means many nights drop below freezing in winter. Carroll County's hazard mitigation data lists four winter weather events between 2015 and early 2019, along with winter storm watches, warnings, and advisories. That is enough cold, a few times a year, to freeze unprotected pipes.

Homes in this part of Georgia were often built with less pipe insulation than homes farther north, because freezes were assumed to be rare. A few cheap steps before the first hard freeze cost far less than a repair after it.
