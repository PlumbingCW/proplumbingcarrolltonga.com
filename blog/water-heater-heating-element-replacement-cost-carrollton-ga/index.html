Cost guide

Water Heater Heating Element Replacement Cost in Carrollton, GA

An electric water heater has two heating elements, and when one fails you get lukewarm water or none. Replacing an element is among the cheaper water heater repairs, but only if the tank around it is sound. This guide explains what moves the price and how to decide between a new element and a new tank.

By the Carrollton Pro Plumbing team

Call (770) 214-4545

How the two elements work

An electric tank has an upper and a lower element. The upper heats the top of the tank first, then switches the power to the lower, which does most of the work. A failed upper element usually means no hot water. A failed lower element means a small amount of hot water that runs out fast.

The elements sit in the water and gradually collect scale. Hard water speeds that up. When the scale insulates the element, it overheats and burns out. Gas water heaters do not have elements. If yours is gas, this repair does not apply, and the symptom points to the pilot, the thermocouple, or the burner.

What a failed element looks like

The symptom tells us which part to test.

What drives the price

Elements are inexpensive. The visit around them is the main variable.

  • The part: standard screw-in elements cost little, specialty or low-density elements cost more.
  • Whether the tank must be drained, which adds time.
  • Whether both elements and the thermostats should be replaced together because the tank is aging.
  • Access: a tank in a tight closet or attic is slower to work on.
  • Time of day: after-hours calls cost more than scheduled visits.
  • Tank age and condition, since an old tank may not be worth the part.

Replace the element or the whole tank?

If the tank is under 10 years old and not leaking, an element replacement is almost always the right call. The Department of Energy says most storage water heaters last 10 to 15 years, so a younger tank has life left. The repair is cheap compared with a new tank.

If the tank is past 12 years, rusted at the base, or has been repaired repeatedly, the new element may buy only a short time. Put the two numbers side by side. See our water heater replacement page for what a new tank involves.

Safety first if you try it yourself

Turn off the power at the breaker, then confirm it with a voltage tester. Water and 240 volts are a dangerous mix. Drain the tank below the element before you remove it, and never power the tank with the element out of the water. A dry-fired element burns out in seconds.

If you are not comfortable testing wiring, call a plumber. A failed thermostat or high-limit switch can look like a failed element, and replacing the wrong part wastes money.

Testing before you buy a part

A multimeter tells you whether an element is bad. With power off at the breaker, a good element shows resistance between its terminals, and an open element shows none. A reading from either terminal to the tank wall means the element has shorted to ground.

Testing the thermostats and the high limit reset button comes first, since a tripped limit looks like a dead element. Skipping that step is the most common reason people replace a part that was fine.

What a good repair visit looks like

The plumber confirms the power is off, drains the tank below the element, removes the old part with an element wrench, fits a new gasket, and tightens the new one. After refilling, they run water until the air is out before restoring power. Turning the power on before the tank is full burns out a new element in seconds.

Ask the plumber to check the anode rod and the tank bottom while the tank is open. If the tank is heavily scaled or leaking at the seams, a new element only delays the inevitable.

Carrollton, GA note: scale and permits

The City of Carrollton's published water quality report did not list a hardness figure. If you see crust on faucet aerators, scale may be shortening your elements' life. Ask the utility or test your tap.

A repair of this kind is a like-for-like part swap. A full tank replacement is a different job, and the City of Carrollton says it issues plumbing and gas permits that are picked up in person by the state card holder. Ask the building office what applies at your address.
