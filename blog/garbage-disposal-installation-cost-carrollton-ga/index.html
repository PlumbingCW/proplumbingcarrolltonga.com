Cost guide

Cost of a New Garbage Disposal and Installation in Carrollton, GA

The price of a garbage disposal installation depends on three things. They are the unit you choose, the state of the plumbing and wiring under your sink, and whether you are replacing or adding. This guide explains each factor so you can compare quotes on equal terms. We do not list prices here because the same job varies widely from house to house.

By the Carrollton Pro Plumbing team

What the disposal itself costs, and why

Disposals are sold by motor size, grind stages, and build quality. Smaller motors handle everyday scraps and cost the least. Larger motors grind tougher waste, run quieter, and last longer. Stainless grinding parts resist rust better than galvanized ones, and some models add sound insulation.

A continuous feed disposal runs while the switch is on. A batch feed starts only when the stopper is turned, and it costs more. Most households do well with a mid-range continuous feed unit. If the old one lasted a decade, buy the same class again.

What changes the installation price

These are the items that move the total, in roughly the order they matter.

  • Replacing an existing disposal is cheaper than adding a first one, because the flange, wiring, and drain are already in place.
  • A first-time install may need an outlet, a switch, and a new drain connection under the sink.
  • The sink flange and mounting kit differ by brand, so a different brand can need a new flange.
  • The dishwasher drain hose connects to the disposal and may need a new connection or an air gap.
  • A cramped cabinet slows the job, and a soft cabinet floor from past leaks adds repair work.
  • A hardwired unit needs a licensed electrician in some jurisdictions, while a cord plug does not.

How to compare quotes honestly

Ask each plumber what is included. A quote that lists the disposal model, the flange, the electrical work, the drain connections, and haul-away of the old unit lets you compare on equal ground. A quote that says only a total hides the differences.

Ask what happens if the cabinet floor or the drain pipe turns out to be damaged. A clear answer in advance prevents a surprise at the end of the job. We give a clear price before work starts and explain the plan if the scope changes.

Care that stretches the life of the new unit

Run cold water during and for 15 seconds after grinding, avoid grease and fibrous scraps like celery and corn husks, and keep eggshells, coffee grounds, and pasta out. A disposal is for soft scraps. Treating it like a trash can shortens its life and clogs the drain beyond it.

If the drain slows after you install a new unit, the problem is probably in the line, not the disposal. Our drain cleaning service clears that.

What the installation visit includes

A straightforward swap takes the old unit off the sink flange, mounts the new one, reconnects the drain trap and the dishwasher inlet, and wires or plugs in the power. The plumber also checks the sink drain for slow flow, since a disposal sends more debris into the line than a bare drain does.

A first-time install costs more in labor because the sink needs a larger flange opening and a power source under the sink. If there is no outlet or switch, an electrician may be needed. Ask who handles that part before you agree to a price.

Questions to ask before you buy a unit

How much horsepower does the unit have, and is that enough for your household? Does the warranty cover parts and labor, and for how long? Is the unit made for septic systems if your home is not on city sewer? Does the quote include the dishwasher knockout and the power connection?

Ask also whether the old unit will be hauled away. A quote that lists these items is easier to compare than one that gives a single number.

Carrollton, GA note: permits and licensed work

According to the City of Carrollton, it issues building, electrical, HVAC, plumbing, gas, and pool permits, and permits are picked up in person by the state card holder. A like-for-like disposal swap usually does not need one, but adding a circuit under the sink may. Ask the building office before work starts if you are adding wiring.

Georgia's licensing portal lets you confirm a plumber's or electrician's license. Check any contractor before you hire them, and ask who is responsible for the permit when one is needed.
