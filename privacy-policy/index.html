Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we receive when you call us or visit this website, and what we do with it.

Call (770) 214-4545

Information we receive by phone

When you call (770) 214-4545, we receive your phone number, the address of the job, and the details you give about the plumbing problem. We use that information to schedule and carry out the service you request and to follow up on it.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By continuing the call you agree to this recording. If you prefer not to be recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

Information from this website

This website does not have forms, accounts, or a checkout. We do not collect names, emails, or payment details through the site. Our web host may record standard technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, in server logs for security and performance.

The pages load fonts from Google Fonts and show maps from Google Maps. Those services receive your IP address and browser information when the content loads, under their own privacy policies. We do not control what they collect.

How we use and share information

We use call information to send a technician, give a price, schedule work, and improve our service. We share it with the technicians and contractors who perform the work and with service providers who help us run our phone line, only as needed for that purpose.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the rights and safety of customers, our contractors, and the public.

Your choices and contact

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to records we must keep by law. Contact us at contact@proplumbingcarrolltonga.com or call (770) 214-4545.

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. We may update this policy, and the updated version will appear on this page with the year of the change, 2026.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies. If we add analytics later, we will describe it here. Your browser may store fonts and images from our pages to load them faster, which is normal and under your control in your browser settings.

Links to outside sites, such as government pages, are covered by their own policies.
