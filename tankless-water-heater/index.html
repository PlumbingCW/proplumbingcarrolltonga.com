Tankless Water Heater Service in Carrollton, GA

A tankless heater warms water as you use it, so there is no tank to run out. It also has parts a tank does not, from flame sensors to heat exchangers that scale up. We install, repair, and descale wall-mounted units in Carrollton and nearby towns.

How tankless heaters differ from tanks

When a hot tap opens, a flow sensor tells the burner to fire and the water passes through a heat exchanger on its way out. There is no stored hot water, so the supply is continuous as long as the unit can keep up with the flow. The limit is how many fixtures run at once.

The Department of Energy says tankless water heaters can last more than 20 years, compared with 10 to 15 for most storage tanks. That lifespan only holds if the heat exchanger is kept clear of scale, which is why maintenance matters more here than on a tank.

Common tankless problems we repair

Error codes point to the system that failed. These are the ones we see most.

  • Cold water sandwich, where hot water goes lukewarm and then hot again mid-shower.
  • Error codes for ignition, flame, or exhaust failure.
  • Reduced flow caused by scale in the heat exchanger or a clogged inlet filter.
  • Condensate drain blockage on high-efficiency condensing models.
  • A failed flow sensor or control board.
  • Venting that has been disturbed, blocked, or corroded.

Descaling, step by step

Descaling is the single most useful service a tankless heater gets. We use a pump and a descaling solution.

  1. Isolate the unit.

    We close the supply valves and open the service ports on the isolation valve kit.

  2. Circulate the solution.

    A small pump moves descaling solution through the heat exchanger for the time the manufacturer specifies.

  3. Flush and clean.

    We rinse the system, clean the inlet screen, and check the condensate drain.

  4. Restart and test.

    We reopen the valves, fire the unit, and confirm temperature and flow.

Why scale matters if you have hard water

The City of Carrollton's published water quality report did not list a hardness figure, so we do not guess at one. If your kettle or faucet aerators crust over with white deposits, you likely have enough mineral content to scale a heat exchanger over time. You can ask the water utility directly or send a sample to a lab.

In that case an annual descale is cheap insurance, and a whole-house softener or filter ahead of the heater helps more. See our plumbing installation page if you want a treatment system added alongside the heater.

Code and permits for this work

Georgia's Department of Community Affairs publishes the state's amendments to the International Plumbing Code (IPC), the standard that plumbing work in Georgia is measured against, and the City of Carrollton issues plumbing permits through its permits office. Tankless units add gas sizing and venting rules, and the maker's manual sets the details the code points to.

What changes the price of tankless work

Tankless jobs vary more than tank swaps because the infrastructure around the unit matters.

  • The unit's capacity and efficiency rating.
  • Gas line size and the length of the run.
  • Venting material and route to the outside.
  • Electrical outlet and condensate drain requirements.
  • Age and condition of the existing isolation valves.
  • Whether the call is repair, descale, or a full installation.

We quote before we start. If your tankless unit is failing, we compare the repair against replacing it with another tankless or a tank.
