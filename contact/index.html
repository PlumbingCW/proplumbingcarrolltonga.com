Contact

Contact Carrollton Pro Plumbing

The fastest way to reach us is the phone. The line is answered 24/7, and a person asks what is happening and where.

Call (770) 214-4545

Call, any hour

The number is (770) 214-4545 and it is open 24/7, nights, weekends, and holidays. We do not use contact forms. A short call is faster, and the dispatcher can tell you what to shut off while a technician is scheduled.

For emergencies such as a burst pipe, a sewage backup, or a leaking water heater, call and say so at the start. Same-day appointments are offered when available for problems that are not urgent.

Our address and email

Carrollton Pro Plumbing, 1670 US Highway 27, Carrollton, GA 30116. The map below shows the location. Our service area includes the city of Carrollton and nearby towns, and we dispatch from here.

You can also reach us by email at contact@proplumbingcarrolltonga.com for non-urgent questions. For anything where water is flowing, call the phone line instead, because email is not monitored for emergencies.

What happens after you call

The dispatcher records your address and the problem, tells you what to shut off or avoid, and schedules a technician. Same-day appointments are offered when available, and for emergencies the line is open all hours.

Before work begins, the technician explains what they found and gives you the price. You decide whether to go ahead.

Other ways to learn about us

Browse the services page to see what we handle, or read the service areas page to confirm we cover your town. The about page explains how we work and how to check a plumber's license in Georgia.

Where our guidance comes from

Our service pages draw on published guidance from the EPA, the US Department of Energy, and the City of Carrollton, and they say where a Georgia code or permit may apply. If a source is not named on a page, treat the statement as general plumbing practice and ask the technician.

What to have ready when you call

A few details help the dispatcher send the right technician with the right equipment.

Your address.

Street address and the nearest crossroads if you are in a rural area.

What is happening.

Where the water or backup is, and how long it has been going on.

What you have done.

Whether you closed the main shutoff or used any drain cleaner.

Fuel or fixture type.

For a water heater, whether it is gas or electric. For a toilet, the room and floor.
